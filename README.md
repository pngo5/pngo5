## 🤖 Autonomous Systems Research

Many market regime models focus on identifying the current regime after the change has already occurred. My research interest focuses on the more difficult problem:

> How can an autonomous system detect that a market regime is beginning to change early enough to adapt, while minimizing false signals, excessive turnover, and unnecessary strategy switching?

Areas of interest include:

* Real-time market regime transition detection
* Probabilistic forecasting and uncertainty estimation
* Change-point detection and hidden-state modeling
* Dynamic strategy selection
* Automated switching between trading strategies
* Multi-agent financial research systems
* Online learning from evolving market conditions
* Risk-aware decision thresholds
* False transition and over-switching prevention
* Performance monitoring and autonomous model evaluation

The goal is not simply to label markets as bullish, bearish, trending, or mean-reverting. The goal is to build systems that can estimate when market behavior is changing, determine how confident they are, and decide whether a strategy adjustment is justified.
